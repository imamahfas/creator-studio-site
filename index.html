Creator Studio DBC

A personal tool that sends the owner's own original short videos to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts.

Connect your TikTok account

The owner connects their own TikTok account once through TikTok Login Kit. The app then reads only the account's display name and open ID, and can upload videos to that account's inbox.

Requested permissions: user.info.basic (display name, open ID) and video.upload (upload videos to the inbox as drafts).

How it works

  1. The owner publishes an original short video on another platform.
  2. The app uploads the same video file to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, Upload, FILE_UPLOAD source).
  3. The owner opens the TikTok notification, reviews the draft, adds the caption and taps Post in the TikTok app.

Nothing is published automatically. There is no Direct Post, no scheduling, and no access to other users' data.

Products and scopes used

  • Login Kit — scope user.info.basic
  • Content Posting API (Upload) — scope video.upload

Content

Videos are the owner's original short stories and facts. AI assistance (voice and animation) and affiliate relationships are disclosed in the captions where applicable.
