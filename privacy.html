Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Creator Studio ("the application") is a personal, single-user tool. This policy explains how it handles data obtained through Google and Facebook account authorization.

What data is accessed

When the owner authorizes the application with their own Google account, it requests permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope) and to read basic public statistics for videos it uploaded. When authorized with the owner's own Facebook account, it requests permission to publish Reels to the owner's own Facebook Page and to read basic public insights for those posts.

The application does not request access to any other person's data, does not read private messages, contacts, or any data unrelated to the owner's own channel/Page content.

How data is stored and used

Authorization tokens are stored only in local files on the owner's own computer (not transmitted to any third-party server, and not accessible to anyone other than the owner). They are used exclusively to let the application upload the owner's own videos and read the owner's own video statistics. No data obtained through these tokens is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Data retention and revocation

The owner may revoke the application's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page or their Facebook Business Integrations settings, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

Contact

This is a personal project with a single owner/operator. Questions can be sent to the contact email registered for this application in the Google Cloud / Meta for Developers console.
